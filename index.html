How adaptive scheduling works

The app calculates remaining effort = estimated hours − completed hours. It ranks work using deadline urgency, difficulty and importance, then assigns focused blocks into free time while respecting your availability blocks and daily limits. If demand exceeds capacity, it reports the shortfall. Recalculate after you add work, log progress or change availability. This is a deterministic local scheduler—not an AI that knows your course material or university LMS.
